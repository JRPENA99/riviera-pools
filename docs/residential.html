01 · Geometric Pools

Classic lines, designed around the house.

Geometric pools feature even lines and clean shapes that complement your home’s architecture and give it a more luxurious feel. They maximise space — ideal for laps and recreation — and can incorporate rock slides, water walls and fire features.

  • Lap & recreation layouts
  • Sun shelves & bubblers
  • Water walls & fire features
Representative imagery
02 · Free-Form Pools

Natural curves, built in stone.

Free-form pools trade hard angles for curves — lagoon-style designs with rock features, waterfalls and stone decking. Popular across Texas for recreation and entertaining, they can add grottos, jump rocks or a natural waterfall slide.

  • Lagoon-style designs
  • Grottos & jump rocks
  • Natural waterfall slides
